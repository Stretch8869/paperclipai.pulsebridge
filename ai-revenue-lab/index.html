FAQ

Built to be useful, not overhyped.

Is this a course or software?

Both. The learning system teaches the method and the AI Tools Hub turns those methods into reusable business assets.

Does this guarantee revenue?

No. The platform gives you frameworks, tools and implementation guidance. Results depend on the market, offer, traffic, pricing, execution and other factors.

How are payments handled?

Starter, Pro and Builder Membership use Stripe-hosted checkout. Stripe handles the payment page and payment method collection.

Is the member demo secure paid access?

No. This public preview is intentionally a demo. Secure production member access requires server-side identity and verified paid entitlements before paid traffic should be scaled.
